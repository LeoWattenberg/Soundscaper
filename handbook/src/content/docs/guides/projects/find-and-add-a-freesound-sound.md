The Freesound panel lets you find a sound without leaving Soundscaper. Search for what you need, check the creator and license shown with each result, and insert the sound where you want it on the timeline. Soundscaper imports the high-quality preview as a project clip.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **File → Import → Audio after downloading a sound from Freesound**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **View → Panels → Freesound**.
3. In the **Freesound** panel, type a word or phrase describing the sound you want into **Search Freesound**, then press **Search**. Matching sounds appear in **Freesound results**.
4. In **Freesound results**, press **Add to project** beside the result you want. The sound appears as a clip at the playhead.
5. The project now shows 1 clip.
   *You should see:* The imported sound on the timeline, ready to edit with the rest of the project.

## Tips

- Check the creator and license links beside a result before you use the sound in something you share.
- **Add to project** puts the sound on the timeline. Move the playhead first if you want the clip to start later.
