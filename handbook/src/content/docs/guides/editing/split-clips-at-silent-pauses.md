## What this guide is for {#what-this-guide-is-for}

Long pauses can make a spoken take easier to edit when each phrase is its own clip. Split clips at silences removes the silent runs and leaves the separated phrases in place, so their timing on the timeline does not change.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Edit → Audio Clips → Detach at Silences (Audacity 3)**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import** and pick the take with pauses between its phrases. It lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Choose **Edit → Audio clips → Split clips at silences**. Each detected silent run is removed from the clip and the phrases on either side become separate clips.
4. The phrases are now separate clips at their original times.
   *You should see:* Each phrase keeps its place in the recording.

## Tips

- This command separates a clip at silence; it does not shorten the gaps between the phrases.
- For pauses you want to keep but shorten, use [Remove silent pauses](/guides/cleaning-up/remove-silent-pauses/) instead.
