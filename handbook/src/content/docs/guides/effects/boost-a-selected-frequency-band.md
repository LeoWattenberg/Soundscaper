## What this guide is for {#what-this-guide-is-for}

Spectral Amplify raises a frequency band only inside a selected time span. A small boost can bring out a quiet tone or detail without raising the entire recording.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Effect → Spectral Tools → Amplify selection (Audacity 4)**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import** and pick the recording with a quiet detail you want to emphasize. It lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Open the menu for the track you want to inspect from the **Track menu** button in its header and choose **Track visualization → Spectrogram**.
4. Drag in the ruler above the clip to select the passage containing the detail.
5. Choose **Effect → Spectral editing → Spectral box select**. Set **Minimum frequency (Hz)** to `200` and **Maximum frequency (Hz)** to `320`, set **Gain (dB)** to `4`, then press **Spectral Amplify**. A modest 4 dB boost raises only the chosen band during this passage.
6. Press **Play** to listen, then **Stop**.
   *You should see:* The selected detail is more prominent in that passage, while the rest stays at its previous level.

## Tips

- Keep the frequency bounds close to the detail you want; a wider band also raises nearby sounds.
- Use a smaller gain if the boosted passage becomes harsh or louder than the surrounding audio.
