The effects in the Effect menu change the audio once and for all. A realtime effect sits on a track instead: it runs during playback and export, its settings can be changed at any time, and the original audio is never touched. It is the right choice for reverb, EQ and compression that you want to keep adjusting as the mix develops.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Effect → Add Realtime Effects**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import audio** and pick the recording the effect should sit on. The file lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Press **Effects** in the track's header to open the Effects panel, press **Effects** in the track's rack and choose **Reverb**. Its settings window opens; press **Close** when you are done with it. The track’s rack now lists Reverb. Its settings window stays available from the rack whenever you want to adjust it.
4. Press **Play** to listen, then **Stop**.
   *You should see:* The track plays through the reverb.

## Tips

- Add several effects to a rack and drag them to change their order; each feeds the next.
- The master rack, at the bottom of the Effects panel, processes the whole mix.
