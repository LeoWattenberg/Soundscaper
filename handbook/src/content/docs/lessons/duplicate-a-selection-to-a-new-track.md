Duplicate copies the selection onto a new track directly below, at the same position in time. It is the usual first step for parallel processing — a heavily compressed copy mixed under the original, a reverb-only track, a doubled vocal.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Edit → Duplicate (Ctrl+D)**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import audio** and pick your recording. This lesson uses `lesson-music-loop.wav`. The file lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Drag in the ruler above the clip, from the quarter point to the three-quarter point of the clip, to select that range. Select the passage to duplicate.
4. Choose **Edit → Duplicate**.
5. The project now shows 2 clips.
   *You should see:* A second clip holding just the selection, on a new track under the first, lined up in time.

## Tips

- Mute one of the two tracks to hear the other on its own while you shape it.
- When you are happy with the blend, [mix the tracks down](/lessons/mix-tracks-into-one/).
