A tutorial is a lesson: it takes you through a complete piece of work on an example recording the handbook provides, step by step, and tells you what you should see along the way. Start here if you have not used Soundscaper before. When you know your way around and have a recording of your own, the [how-to guides](/guides/) cover the same operations and many more, one task at a time.

- [Your first Soundscaper project](/tutorials/your-first-project/) — Import a recording, listen, split it, fade it out, export a file and save the project.
- [Clean up a voice recording](/tutorials/clean-up-a-voice-recording/) — Remove hum from a take, cut the rumble, bring it to podcast loudness and export an MP3.
- [Layer music under a voice](/tutorials/put-music-under-a-voice/) — Combine two tracks, duck one under the other automatically, mix them down and export.

<summary>How this tutorial stays current</summary>

Each tutorial is replayed on its example files, step for step, against each new version of Soundscaper by the browser suite (`tests/browser/soundscaper-tutorials.spec.js`). If a step stops working, it will fail the build rather than becoming outdated.
