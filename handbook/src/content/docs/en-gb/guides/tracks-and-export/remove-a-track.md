## What this guide is for {#what-this-guide-is-for}

Remove tracks deletes the selected track and all the clips it contains from the project. It is useful for an unused take or a spare copy after comparing edits. Selecting one clip targets its entire track, so check the selection before removing anything.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity’s **Tracks → Remove tracks (Audacity 4)**. The names below are Soundscaper’s own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import** and pick the recording to keep. It lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Choose **File → Import** and pick the unwanted recording on another track. It lands as a clip on its own track.
4. Use **Tab** to focus a clip on the track to remove and press **Enter** to select it.
5. Choose **Tracks → Remove tracks**.
6. The recording you kept is still at its original position.
   *You should see:* The unwanted track is gone, and the other recording is untouched.

## Tips

- **Edit → Undo** restores the removed track and its clips.
- To remove only part of a recording, [delete a passage and leave a gap](/guides/editing/delete-a-passage-and-leave-a-gap/) instead of deleting its track.
- Removing a track from the project does not delete the original file you imported from your computer.
- Tracks in a linked lane group are removed together.
