## What this guide is for {#what-this-guide-is-for}

Risset Drum combines a decaying tone with a band of noise to make a synthetic percussion hit. Adjust its decay for a short accent or a longer, more resonant sound.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Generate → Risset Drum (Nyquist)**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **Generate → Nyquist → Risset Drum**. In the **Risset Drum** dialog, set **Frequency (Hz)** to `100`, set **Decay (seconds)** to `1.5` and set **Amplitude (0 - 1)** to `0.5`, then press **Apply**. A 1.5-second decay keeps the hit compact; the moderate amplitude leaves room to mix it with other sounds.
3. The project now shows 1 clip.
   *You should see:* A non-silent percussive clip about 1.5 seconds long appears on the timeline.

## Tips

- Lower **Frequency (Hz)** for a deeper hit; raise it for a brighter, higher sound.
- Increase **Amount of noise in mix (percent)** for a noisier attack.
