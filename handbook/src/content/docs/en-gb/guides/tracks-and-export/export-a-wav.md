WAV preserves every sample precisely as the editor displays it. Utilise it for masters, when passing audio to a video editor or another DAW, and for any situation where you may wish to reprocess the audio.

:::note[Transitioning from Audacity?]
This is the equivalent of **File → Export Audio → WAV** in Audacity. The names used below are specific to Soundscaper and may vary.
:::

## Instructions

1. Launch Soundscaper. A new, blank project will be ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Select **File → Import** and choose the recording you wish to deliver. It will be placed as a clip on its own track.
3. Choose **File → Export audio**, set **Format** to **WAV**, and click **Export**. The file will download once the rendering process is complete, and a link to it will remain in the dialog box.

## Additional Advice

- WAV files are large, approximately ten megabytes per minute for stereo at CD quality.
- To export each track as a separate file, adjust the **Mode** in the export dialog to 'individual stems'.
