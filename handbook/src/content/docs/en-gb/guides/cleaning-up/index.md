Remove noise, clicks, rumble, and dead air from a recording before you begin editing.

## Guides in this category

- [Eliminate background noise](/guides/cleaning-up/remove-background-noise/) — Train the Noise Reduction tool to recognise background hum, then remove it from the entire recording.
- [Eliminate clicks and pops](/guides/cleaning-up/remove-clicks-and-pops/) — Remove short, sharp clicks from a recording without affecting the rest of the audio.
- [Reduce long pauses](/guides/cleaning-up/remove-silent-pauses/) — Tighten a recording by trimming every long silence to a consistent short gap.
- [Mute a section of audio](/guides/cleaning-up/silence-part-of-a-recording/) — Replace a portion of audio with silence while maintaining the original timing.
- [Remove low-frequency rumble](/guides/cleaning-up/remove-low-rumble/) — Reduce sub-bass thumps from traffic, wind, and handling using a high-pass filter.
- [Correct DC offset](/guides/cleaning-up/fix-dc-offset/) — Centre an audio waveform that is shifted above or below the zero line.
