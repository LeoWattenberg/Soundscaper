## What this guide is for {#what-this-guide-is-for}

Two identical, time-aligned recordings cancel when one has its polarity inverted and they play together. This check can reveal whether edits or processing made a copy differ from its source.

:::note[Coming from Audacity?]
This is Audacity's **Effect → Special → Invert**. The names below are Soundscaper's own, which sometimes differ.
:::

## Steps

1. Open Soundscaper. A new, empty project is ready as soon as the editor loads.
2. Choose **File → Import** and pick the recording you want to compare with a copy. It lands as a clip on its own track.
3. Choose **Select → All**.
4. Open the menu for the track you are comparing from the **Track options** button in its header and choose **Duplicate track**.
5. Choose **Effect → Special → Invert**. The effect applies to the selection straight away. Inverting the copy makes matching samples opposite in polarity while the original remains unchanged.
6. Press **Play** to listen, then **Stop**.
   *You should see:* If the tracks match and remain aligned, they cancel; any remaining sound reveals a difference.

## Tips

- Cancellation requires the same timing and level; even a small offset leaves an audible residual.
- Keep both tracks unmuted and leave Solo off so both can be heard together. Undo restores the duplicate before the polarity test.
