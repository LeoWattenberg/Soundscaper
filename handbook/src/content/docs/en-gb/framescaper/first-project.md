## 1. Import a video

Select **File → Import** and choose a supported video file. The imported video and its extracted audio will appear in the Project Bin as a single media item, typically arranged on adjacent linked lanes.

The original video is saved as project media, while posters and filmstrip thumbnails are disposable previews that can be regenerated as needed.

## 2. Review source and sequence

Utilise the Source Monitor to examine your material before placement or adjustment. The Video Preview is ideal for inspecting the composed sequence.

Linked picture and audio edits are synchronised by default, moving, splitting, trimming, and stretching together. Ensure you review both picture and sound across each edit by playing through them.

## 3. Arrange and finish

Employ timeline edit commands for standard trims and moves. Framescaper also provides access to video effects, geometry adjustments, compositing options, nested sequences, and multicamera commands when applicable to the current selection.

Save your local project, then proceed to [video export](/framescaper/video-export/).
