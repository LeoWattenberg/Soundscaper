Soundscaper is a local-first editor for recording, repairing, arranging, mixing, analysing and delivering audio. This handbook explains those workflows with task-focused guides and complete example projects.

## Tutorials

A tutorial is a lesson: it takes you through a complete piece of work on an example recording the handbook provides, step by step, and tells you what you should see along the way. Start here if you have not used Soundscaper before.

- [Your first Soundscaper project](/tutorials/your-first-project/) — Import a recording, listen to it, split it, fade it out, export a file and save the project.
- [Clean up a voice recording](/tutorials/clean-up-a-voice-recording/) — Take the hum out of a recording, cut the rumble, bring it to podcast loudness and export an MP3.
- [Put music under a voice](/tutorials/put-music-under-a-voice/) — Layer two tracks, duck one under the other automatically, mix them down and export.

See [all tutorials](/tutorials/).

## How-to guides

Each guide takes one task and shows how to do it in Soundscaper on your own recording, with the exact menu entries and dialog fields to use. The guides are grouped by what you are trying to do.

- [Cleaning up a recording](/guides/cleaning-up/) — Take noise, clicks, rumble and dead air out of a recording before you work on it.
- [Volume and dynamics](/guides/volume/) — Set levels, even out the loud and quiet parts, and hit a delivery target.
- [Editing](/guides/editing/) — Cut, split, copy, move and mark up material on the timeline.
- [Effects](/guides/effects/) — Change the character of a sound: pitch, tempo, space, filtering and distortion.
- [Tracks and export](/guides/tracks-and-export/) — Work with several tracks and render the result to a file.
- [Projects and files](/guides/projects/) — Save, move and open projects, including projects made in Audacity.
- [Analysis](/guides/analysis/) — Measure loudness, inspect frequencies and find problems in a recording.

See [all how-to guides](/guides/).
